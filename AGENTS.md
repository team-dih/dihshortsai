# AGENTS.md

## Project Overview

An AI-powered SaaS for generating short-form chat conversation videos
for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok.

## Core Features

- Configure two participants with names, profile pictures, and AI voices.
- Create and edit messages between the participants.
- Select Minecraft gameplay from a background video library.
- Generate AI voiceovers for each message.
- Preview and export the final video as an MP4.

## Video Composition

- Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio (1080 × 1920).
- Fullscreen Minecraft gameplay as the background.
- Floating iMessage-style chat interface over the gameplay.
- Messages appear sequentially, synchronized with AI voiceovers.
- Incoming messages use gray bubbles; outgoing messages use blue bubbles.
- Automatically scroll the conversation as new messages appear.

## Development Principles

- Keep the UI modern, minimal, and intuitive.
- Maintain clean, modular, and reusable code.
- Prioritize video quality, performance, and usability.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity and overengineering.
